# 🧠 MindLock

**MindLock** is a time management and productivity app where our brains meet time management.

---

## 📌 The Concept

- ⏱️ Study Timer Session
- 📊 Progress analytics
- ✅ Task management
- 🔒 Distraction locking
- 🎧 Spotify Links
- 💬 ChatGPT
- ☁️ Cloud Storage
- ✍️ Google and Apple Sign in
